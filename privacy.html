Who we are

OA-Operation Application ("the app") is provided by Green Tech Cambodia. Contact: greentechnology.cambodia@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and phone number, and your PIN (used to sign in).
  • Work records entered by you or your organization: attendance, leave requests, payslip and payroll information, HR records and company asset records.
  • Location: if you use check-in, the app reads your device's precise location at the moment you check in or out. The app does not track your location in the background.

How we use information

We use this information only to provide the app to your organization: to sign you in, record attendance, process leave requests, show payslips and manage assets. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Who can see your information

You can see your own records. Administrators of your organization can see records for their organization. We use hosting and infrastructure providers to store and process data on our behalf. We may disclose information if required by law.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept while your organization uses the app. You can request access, correction or deletion of your data by emailing greentechnology.cambodia@gmail.com. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

Data is transmitted over encrypted connections and access is protected by your personal PIN. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

The app is intended for working adults and is not directed to children under 13.
